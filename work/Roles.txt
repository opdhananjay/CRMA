1. Super Admin
Platform level
Manages the entire CRM platform.

Responsibilities
- Manage organizations and subscriptions.
- Activate or deactivate organizations.
- Manage platform-level configuration.
- Monitor platform health.
- Manage platform administrators.
Example: Anjaya Systems platform administrator.



2. Manager / Organization Admin
Business level
Manages their own organization's CRM.

Responsibilities
- Manage organization users.
- Create, assign and reassign leads.
- View all organization leads.
- Manage lead types, sources and statuses.
- View team performance and reports.
- Manage follow-ups and activities.
Example: Owner or sales manager of a real estate agency.



3. Agent / Employee
Business level
Handles assigned leads and follow-ups.

Responsibilities
- View assigned leads.
- Add calls, notes and activities.
- Schedule follow-ups.
- Update lead status.
- Manage their own daily tasks.
- View personal performance.
